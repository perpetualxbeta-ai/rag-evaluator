# Frequently Asked Questions

> **Note:** Fictional sample data for rag-evaluator.

## Can Kestrel K2 robots work in our freezer?

No. The K2's operating temperature range is 0 °C to 40 °C, so it is suited to ambient and chilled areas only. Operating it in a freezer is outside the specifications and is not covered by the warranty.

## How long does a battery last, and how do we know when to replace it?

A battery typically powers the robot for about 10 hours on a single charge, and much longer when opportunity charging is used. Over its lifetime the battery is rated for 3,000 full charge cycles. On HalcyonOS 3.3 or later, Halcyon Fleet shows each battery's state of health and warns you when it drops below 75%. Replace the battery before its state of health reaches 70%. If that happens within three years and 3,000 cycles, the replacement is covered by the battery warranty.

## Our robots keep stopping in the same aisle. What should we do?

Repeated stops in the same place usually mean something is entering the safety scanners' protective field. Check the aisle for obstructions, and clean the scanner windows with isopropyl alcohol and a lint-free cloth; ammonia-based cleaners leave a film that causes this problem. Before HalcyonOS 3.2, reflective floor tape could also cause unnecessary stops, so updating the firmware may help. If the stops continue, contact Halcyon support.

## Can we make robots go faster near our packing stations?

Packing stations are normally shared zones. On HalcyonOS 3.2 or later, your site administrator can raise the shared-zone limit for a zone up to 1.5 m/s. It cannot go higher, regardless of plan or configuration. On earlier firmware the limit is fixed at 1.2 m/s.

## Can one fleet serve several floors?

Yes, from HalcyonOS 3.1 onwards, provided your freight elevators are integrated with Halcyon Fleet. Elevator integration is set up by Halcyon engineers during deployment or as a follow-up project.

## What's the difference between Standard and Premium support?

Premium support is available around the clock, includes a phone hotline, and responds to critical issues within 2 hours, compared with 8 business hours on Standard. Premium also includes an on-site technician within 48 hours in the EU and continental US, and a quarterly remote fleet health review. Both plans require robots to be running HalcyonOS 3.1 or later.

## We still have Kestrel K1 robots. Are they supported?

Yes. K1 robots receive security updates until the end of 2027 and remain eligible for both support plans. The K1 carries up to 150 kg, half the K2's 300 kg payload, so check load weights if you run mixed fleets.

## Who is allowed to replace drive wheels?

Wheel replacement involves opening the robot's covers, so it must be done by a technician with the Kestrel Maintenance Certification, after a lockout. Drive wheels are replaced as a pair when tread falls below 4 mm, which is checked every 500 operating hours. Drive wheels are wear parts and are not covered by the robot warranty.
