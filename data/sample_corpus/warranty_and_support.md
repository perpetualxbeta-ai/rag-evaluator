# Warranty and Support

> **Note:** Fictional sample data for rag-evaluator. These are not real contract terms.

## Robot warranty

Every new Kestrel K2 comes with a **three-year warranty** covering parts and labour, starting on the date the robot is first put into production at the customer's site. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, including the drive motors, electronics, sensors, and chassis.

Wear parts are not covered by the robot warranty. These are the drive wheels, the caster wheels, and the DockPoint charger contacts, which are replaced during routine maintenance.

## Battery warranty

The battery has its own warranty. It is covered for **three years or 3,000 full charge cycles, whichever comes first**. A battery is eligible for replacement under warranty if its state of health (SoH) falls below 70% within that period.

Battery SoH is read from Halcyon Fleet for robots on HalcyonOS 3.3 or later. For older firmware, a technician's manual measurement is needed to support a claim.

## What voids the warranty

The warranty does not cover damage or failures caused by:

- Skipping the maintenance schedule in the Maintenance Guide. Halcyon may ask for maintenance records when assessing a claim.
- Fitting non-genuine spare parts. This voids the warranty for the affected component only.
- Operating outside the specifications, for example in freezer areas below 0 °C or with loads above 300 kg.
- Collisions caused by disabling or bypassing safety functions.

## Support plans

All customers get access to the online knowledge base and the Halcyon Fleet status page. Beyond that, customers choose one of two support plans:

| | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Hours | Business days, 08:00–18:00 local time of the customer's regional office (Rotterdam, Austin or Singapore) | 24/7, all year |
| Channels | Email and support portal | Email, support portal, and phone hotline |
| Response time, critical issues | 8 business hours | 2 hours |
| Response time, other issues | 2 business days | 8 hours |
| On-site technician | Scheduled visit, quoted separately | Within 48 hours in the EU and the continental US; within 72 hours elsewhere |
| Remote fleet health review | — | Quarterly |

A critical issue is one where more than 25% of a site's fleet cannot operate, or where there is a safety incident.

Support is only provided for robots running HalcyonOS 3.1 or later. Customers on older firmware are asked to update first.

## Kestrel K1 customers

The Kestrel K1 is no longer sold, but K1 robots remain eligible for Standard and Premium support and receive security updates until the end of 2027. New K1 spare parts are available while stocks last.

## Making a claim

Raise warranty claims through the support portal. Include the robot's serial number, its operating hours from Halcyon Fleet, a description of the fault, and, for battery claims, the current SoH reading. Halcyon aims to decide on a claim within five business days.
